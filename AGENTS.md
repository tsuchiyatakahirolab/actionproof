# WebMCP Challenge — project instructions

## Objective and source of truth

Aim for an exceptional, demonstrably useful entry and a plausible TSUCHIYA LAB business, not submission completion alone. Current release scope is in `WINNING_SPEC_v0.7.md`; the final recording plan is in `submission/VIDEO_SCRIPT.md`. Earlier specifications and paper demos are historical context, not active implementation instructions.

## Mandatory concept gates

1. A real, strong pain: existing human time, money, or failure-risk burden, supported by evidence.
2. A one-sentence difference from the strongest relevant alternatives. AI, low price, OSS, and browser support alone do not qualify.
3. Material WebMCP necessity: removing WebMCP must break or substantially degrade the demonstrated experience. Compare with a competent alternative, not an intentionally crippled baseline.
4. A viewer without domain background can understand the problem within 30 seconds.
5. A clear, meaningful before/after change is visible within 60–90 seconds.
6. Every official judging criterion has a specific evidence artifact and a corresponding demonstration scene.

Unknown is not PASS. Do not compensate for a failed gate with a high score elsewhere. Separate a concept-review leader from a selected implementation and from a validated product. Do not invent win probabilities, market demand, customer quotations, benchmark results, or completed tests.

## Demo first

Complete a 60–90 second paper demo before implementation. Build only the selected concept after its pre-build conditions are resolved. Features absent from the video are deferred except prerequisites for correctness, privacy, security, accessibility, testing, or deployment. These prerequisites must have a named reason, not become an unlimited scope exception.

## Judge audit, separate from technical audit

Before submission, a reviewer unfamiliar with the project watches only its first 20 seconds, without its project description, and answers:

- Whose problem is it?
- What happened?
- Why is it better than existing methods?
- Why WebMCP?

If any answer is missing or materially wrong, the audit fails. A self-review or a reviewer who has read the specification is not a blind audit. Record actual responses; do not substitute the script's intended answers. Use a new reviewer for a meaningful retest where possible. Do not create a new user-owned task or delegate without applicable authorization.

## Evidence and safety

- Compare with current official product docs and, before superiority claims, an actual fair baseline test.
- Official specification discussions are not proof of deployed vulnerabilities or paying demand.
- Synthetic data, seeded defects, prerecorded traces, and known-good builds must be labeled.
- Native WebMCP execution, direct JavaScript calls, polyfills, and replays must be distinguishable.
- No destructive tests on third-party or production systems. Use owned or explicitly authorized disposable fixtures with fake data.
- Do not access private research assets, customer data, manuscripts, or accounts without permission. Do not treat tool hints or model judgments as authorization.
- Preserve all user work. Local planning is not authorization for outreach, purchases, external publication, or submission.
